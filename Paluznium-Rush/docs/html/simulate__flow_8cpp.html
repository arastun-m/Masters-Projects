Simulates the flow of materials through a circuit of processing units.

Simulate the flow of materials through a circuit of processing units.

This function performs an iterative simulation of material flow through a set of units, routing outputs to other units or to product/tailings streams, until convergence is reached or the maximum number of iterations is exceeded.

Parameters
unitsVector of Unit objects representing the circuit.
feed_indexIndex of the unit receiving the external feed.
outOutputStreams structure to accumulate product and tailings results.
max_itersMaximum number of iterations to perform.
tolConvergence tolerance for the simulation.
debugIf true, enables verbose output for debugging.
